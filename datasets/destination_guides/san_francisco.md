# San Francisco Travel Guide

## Overview
San Francisco offers waterfront views, diverse neighborhoods, and access to Napa and Marin nature day trips.

## Food
- Ferry Building Marketplace: local artisans and oysters.
- Mission District: burritos, Latin fusion, craft coffee.

## Nature
- Golden Gate Park and Lands End coastal trails.
- Muir Woods redwoods (reservation required).

## Culture
- SFMOMA and de Young Museum.
- Historic cable cars and Alcatraz tours.

## Transportation
- Muni passes; limited parking — prefer transit or rideshare.

*Estimates only — no bookings.*
