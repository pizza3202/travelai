# Japan Travel Guide

## Overview
Japan blends ancient culture, exceptional food, and diverse nature from alpine regions to coastal islands. Tokyo and Kyoto are primary hubs for first-time visitors.

## Food
- **Tsukiji / Toyosu**: seafood markets and sushi breakfasts.
- **Ramen streets**: Shinjuku and Kyoto Station areas.
- **Nishiki Market (Kyoto)**: street food and local specialties.

## Nature
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (Kyoto): iconic bamboo forest walk.
- **Fushimi Inari Shrine**: hillside torii gate hike.
- **Hakone / Fuji day trips**: onsen and lake views near Tokyo.

## Culture
- **Senso-ji Temple** (Asakusa): historic Buddhist temple.
- **Meiji Shrine** (Tokyo): Shinto shrine in forested park.
- **Gion district** (Kyoto): traditional architecture and geiko culture.

## Transportation
- JR Pass for intercity bullet trains; IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) for metros.
- Shinkansen connects Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka efficiently.

## Budget Tips
- Convenience store meals reduce food costs.
- Capsule hotels and business hotels offer mid-range stays in cities.
